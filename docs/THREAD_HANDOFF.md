## Current direction (source-derived summary, not new approvals)

- **Separate and complementary products:** SkyFire SDS for chemical/SDS records, SkyFire Education for learning, SkyFire Safety for public field safety reference; avoid building a rival Lynx work-management platform. Any collaboration/integration is a future discussion.
- **Worker experience:** Read approved, exact manufacturer SDS documents without individual login; find them by name/manufacturer search, supplemental QR code, or site/work-area browse. Support offline copies after successful sync, personal and company devices, and printed SDSs/binders. No worker should be required to own a phone.
- **Administration:** Authorized users manage actual PDF uploads, revisions, approved publication, site/work-area associations, printable QR labels and physical binders, and actionable missing/inconsistent-document indicators. Never imply an unverified record is a verified SDS or that a verified SDS automatically authorizes chemical use.
- **Worker reports:** Flag an existing discrepancy or report a missing/new chemical through two paths (propose basic product information, or flag for administrator identification). Permit photos/video. Keep pending submissions with the reporter and authorized reviewers rather than showing every draft publicly. Worker account/identity for reporting is a **WORKING** design, with enrollment and shared-device security still OPEN; ordinary SDS reading remains login-free.
- **Notifications:** Administrative review alerts and worker-facing chemical hazard/update information are desired; targeted in-app notices are a working starting point. Push notifications are a later-phase exploration, not a currently approved build deliverable.
- **Operational safeguards:** Maintain correct original documents, no AI-generated replacement SDS, QR labels as a supplement rather than replacement for regulated labels, auditability, backups, and a reliable offline/paper fallback. Site/operator policies and detailed MSHA/OSHA compliance interpretation require separate verification; software is not itself proof of HazCom compliance.

## Confirmed November 22 decision and active discovery step

**CONFIRMED D-022:** Mandatory administrator safety triage is the first stage for every newly reported chemical. The reviewer records whether the chemical is **in use**, **present/stored not known in use**, or **unknown**, considers potential worker exposure, and directs prompt Safety/supervisor communication when warranted. Triage is not proof of chemical-use authorization and is not a substitute for the mine's real-world hazard response. Detailed escalation ownership and stop-work rules are not prescribed here.

**CONFIRMED D-023:** One master chemical/product record for each **verified exact manufacturer/product identity**; separate assignments and use-authorization status per mine site/work area. Search for duplicates across sites, but do not automatically merge potential matches or treat approval at one site as approval at another.

**CONFIRMED D-024:** One authorized administrator is sufficient to verify and publish a complete, correct manufacturer SDS with documented product match, provenance, reviewer, date and version history. Unresolved discrepancies stay pending; a routine second approver is **not** required. Whether exceptions mandate second review is OPEN. Document verification remains separate from operator permission to use a chemical.

**CONFIRMED D-025:** A verified SDS remains accessible in the worker library even when site-specific authorization to use that chemical has not been confirmed. Show **SDS verified** and **use authorization unconfirmed** as separate concepts; never imply document publication permits use.

**CONFIRMED D-026:** Workers should have a quick action on a chemical/SDS record to report **observed use, presence at a work area, or a wrong/uncertain status**. Prefill known product/location, allow brief optional context, and route to admin review without directly changing authorization. Potentially unsafe/unapproved use flows through D-022 safety triage; identity and offline submission constraints remain unresolved.

**CONFIRMED D-027:** Workers can report observed chemical use or a potentially incorrect status in **two actions:** select “Report chemical in use” from the chemical/SDS record, then “Submit” on a concise confirmation view. Prefill product/site/area where known, permit location correction, make notes/photos optional, and issue a receipt only after server confirmation. This is a pending administrator report, not an automatic change to authorization or inventory. Worker attribution and offline delivery remain unresolved.

**CONFIRMED D-028:** Worker-reported chemical use is immediately trackable as **“Observed use — pending verification”** and subject to safety triage, but an administrator must verify the actual product and location before changing an official inventory/site assignment record. Verified use is not itself proof of chemical-use authorization; that status remains subject to operator control. Keep audit and disposition history.

**CONFIRMED D-029:** Administrator closeout requires a documented investigation outcome, with cases such as verified use, not found, corrected product identification, or further investigation needed (which stays open). Upon actual resolution, automatically notify the submitting worker with a short outcome. Confirmed use with **unresolved site-use authorization** leaves a separately tracked concern even if the original observed-use report is closed; no automatic approval or silent disappearance. Exact escalation/notification channel is still OPEN.

**CONFIRMED D-030:** New SDS and material hazard-change notices target relevant **sites/work areas by default**, with administrator ability to widen the audience; avoid global notices for trivial edits. Supervisor notices may differ from field-worker notices. Notifications do not substitute for direct hazard communication or training.

**CONFIRMED D-031:** Signed-in user profiles should support **site, department/work area, role, and supervisor/reporting relationship** for differentiated routing and oversight. Admins should be able to grant supervisors selected SDS administrative functions; scope and exact grantable powers remain to be designed. Approved SDS lookup remains login-free.

**CONFIRMED D-032:** Owner approved three base roles (**Field User / Supervisor / Admin**) with **individual, scoped, auditable, revocable administrative permissions** that Admins can grant to supervisors instead of a single full-Admin toggle. The specific actions each role can perform, the default Supervisor baseline, and grant authority remain OPEN. SDS-app permissions do not establish operator chemical-use approval authority; approved SDS lookup remains login-free.

**CONFIRMED D-033:** Supervisor defaults include visibility for assigned teams/work areas, relevant action alerts, acknowledgment, investigation notes and escalation, not automatic SDS publication, official record edits or chemical-use approval.

**CONFIRMED D-034:** Individually configurable grants and restrictions apply **across Field User, Supervisor and Admin profiles**, including department-scoped cross-coverage and proportionate response to misuse. Preserve SDS access and protected safety-reporting channels; permissions and changes need auditability and privileged grant boundaries. Exact controls and restrictions remain OPEN. Do not treat a potentially untrustworthy reporter as grounds for silencing genuine hazard complaints.

**CONFIRMED D-035:** A temporary user-specific permission grant has an explicit expiry and automatically stops granting its privileges at expiration; a justified permanent grant may instead be chosen deliberately. Scope grants by user, permitted actions, and site/department/work area. Maintain audit trail and do not remove unrelated active permissions. Specific default duration, cross-grant precedence, privileged grant authority and offline security enforcement remain OPEN.

**CONFIRMED D-036:** Protected admin writes (controlled SDS publication/revisions, authoritative inventory assignments, permission changes) require a **current online authorization check at acceptance**, not merely a cached login or expired delegated grant. Verified, previously synced SDS lookup remains available offline without login. Workers can save a local draft report offline, visibly labeled **“Saved locally — not yet received”** until server acknowledgment. Urgent hazards must be reported directly to Safety/supervision. Precise offline sync and submission/retry design is still OPEN.

**CONFIRMED D-037:** Offline worker report drafts should automatically retry transmission once connectivity and supported app execution resume. Keep an explicit **locally saved / transmitting / received** distinction; accept a submission as received only on server acknowledgment. Verify the reporting worker's identity, retain unsent drafts if reauthentication is needed, and prevent duplicate creation on retries. Do not promise background sync while a PWA is closed/suspended, and do not replace immediate Safety/supervisor hazard notification.

**CONFIRMED D-038:** Worker report attribution has **two separate fields**: the **account/shared-kiosk session actually used** (plus appropriate audit metadata) and the **actual reporter as self-identified** if different. A typed name is self-reported, not authenticated identity; preserve mismatches for follow-up rather than silently attributing the report to someone else. Support personal devices and mine-owned shared devices; a device per worker is an aspiration, not a requirement. Never store passwords or secrets as audit data, and do not normalize other people's password sharing. This **supersedes the hard requirement in D-037 to independently verify the actual human before accepting every report**; server receipt remains necessary before marking submitted. Admin writes still require stronger authenticated authorization per D-036.

**CONFIRMED D-039:** The ordinary report form requires a **reporter name for shared/kiosk sessions**, while an individually signed-in person's name is **prefilled but editable**. Keep the account/session used separate from the self-identified actual reporter, and do not mislabel typed names as verified identity. Retain low-friction reporting and alternate direct safety channels.

**CONFIRMED D-040:** Authorized SDS administrators must be able to configure site-specific arrangements (one shared shop device, many shared devices, individual devices, or mixed), report fields, help text and dropdown terminology/options. This should be a configuration-driven product, not bespoke code per mine. Preserve immutable system meaning/audit identifiers, login-free SDS access and safeguards; exact configurable field schema, required fields and scope/inheritance remain OPEN.

**CONFIRMED D-041:** Administrators may tailor report wording, optional inputs and local categories, but must **not remove or redefine core safety/traceability behavior**: login-free verified SDS access, reporter/session attribution and identity confidence, product or explicitly unknown product, site/location or unknown location, stable receipt/audit and safety triage history, and distinct SDS document verification versus chemical-use authorization. Local labels map to stable internal states. Do not let configured mandatory questions block genuine safety reporting.

**CONFIRMED D-042:** Published worker-report forms must have controlled configuration versions. Changing dropdown wording, choices or other form settings applies prospectively; preserve the exact prior designation and meaning on already submitted reports, along with the historical form version. Keep stable internal IDs/mappings and audit amendments without overwriting original responses. If meanings actually change, distinguish a new identifier rather than silently repurposing the old one.

**CONFIRMED D-043:** An offline worker report composed using an earlier approved form version should submit with that version and its original answers once connectivity returns, without forced re-entry or automatic relabeling. New safety questions prompt follow-up on the received report rather than discarding the original observation. Preserve identity/session audit, duplicate prevention and server-confirmed receipt; malformed/unsafe payload handling remains OPEN.

**CONFIRMED D-044:** Publication of a revised, approved manufacturer SDS automatically creates distribution/update tracking for affected managed/registered device libraries and physical SDS binders. Connected devices synchronize when possible; offline devices retain their cached versions with document revision and last-sync information. **Never mark digital distribution complete without confirmed sync/integrity or paper binders complete merely because they were generated/printed**; binder replacement requires a recorded confirmation. Dashboard shows pending/failed/unknown gaps. Unregistered personal devices and exact sync mechanics remain OPEN; no guarantee of instantaneous background refresh or operator compliance.

**CONFIRMED D-045:** SDS revision distribution gaps receive **risk-based handling**. Routine document updates remain in the administrator action queue with configurable follow-up timing; significant new/changed hazards with incomplete delivery prompt priority alerts to responsible SDS/HazCom administrators and affected supervisors and indicate where **direct field hazard communication** may be needed. Never infer receipt by offline devices or treat notices as proof of legal HazCom communication. Criteria/authority for identifying a significant revision, deadlines and escalation responsibility remain OPEN.

**CONFIRMED D-046:** One authorized SDS administrator records whether a verified manufacturer SDS revision is **routine/administrative**, **safety-significant**, or **uncertain—requires further review**, with rationale, reviewer, date, and document version. Safety-significant changes trigger D-045 priority follow-up; uncertain changes cannot silently default to routine. No routine second approver required. Exact criteria and handling of uncertain classification at publication remain OPEN.

**CONFIRMED D-047:** If a complete manufacturer SDS is verified for the correct product but the significance of changes remains uncertain, **publish the verified updated SDS for worker access** while recording an unresolved significance review and treating its distribution/administrator-supervisor notice as **high priority** pending review. Preserve the prior controlled revision, mark unupdated offline copies accurately, and never equate document availability with chemical-use approval. If the document itself cannot be verified, D-024 still requires pending review rather than publication.
